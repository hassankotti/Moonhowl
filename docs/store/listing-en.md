## Full description (both stores, ≤4000 chars)
A night of wolves on your Wi-Fi.

Moonhowl is a social deduction party game for 4 to 16 friends in the same room. One phone hosts, everyone else joins over the local Wi-Fi, and the whole game runs between your phones: no internet, no accounts, no sign-up.

HOW IT WORKS
• The host opens a room. Friends find it automatically, scan its QR code, or type its address.
• Everyone gets a secret role. Wolves know each other; the village has to work out who they are.
• Night falls: wolves pick a victim, the Seer peeks at a role, the Guardian protects, the Witch heals or poisons.
• Day breaks: discuss, accuse, and vote someone out. Last side standing wins.

ROLES
Villager, Wolf, Seer, Guardian, Witch, Hunter, Mayor and Jester. Roles are balanced automatically for your group size, or the host picks them.

MADE FOR PLAYING TOGETHER
• A built-in narrator tells every player what to do, or the host can narrate without a role.
• Timers for every phase, with pause and skip for the host.
• Chat: village square, a secret wolf den, a graveyard for the dead, and private messages.
• Lost Wi-Fi for a moment? You return to the same seat and role.
• Sounds, vibration on your turn, and a dark, moonlit interface.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your profile lives only on your phone. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Available in Arabic and English.
